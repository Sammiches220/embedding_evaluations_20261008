What the analogy does not establish

Complexity and travel are separate channels. Height in the atlas’s dimension relief means many local directions. A chosen destination and graph costs are needed to assess travel. A visually impressive peak does not establish cultural value, semantic importance, or inevitable navigation difficulty.
