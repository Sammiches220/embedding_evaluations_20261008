How the map was constructed

Atlas integrity

Disconnected components occupy disjoint chart areas. Every mapped road follows real retained edges. Terrain interpolation is bounded separately within each island. The layout cannot preserve every high-dimensional relation.

Geometric recovery

These checks concern geometry, not a human answering questions about the map. The interface itself provides connectivity and terrain cues automatically.

Synthetic mechanism checks

Planted mechanism names are used only after mapping, to check measurements. They are never used to position or color the atlas.

Interpreting the map

Water means no route in the retained graph, not impossibility in the complete embedding space. Density contours interpolate original-space inverse-radius percentiles. Relief height interpolates measured local dimension, route alternatives, or metric variation. Local spectra expose the directions lost in two axes. Fog shows resampling/compression disagreement; purple seams mark persistent cut candidates. These encodings use explicit thresholds documented in the project.

Roads are a simplified connected backbone plus selected alternate crossings. Unshown graph edges still exist. A marked pass has low cell-crossing conductance, high path centrality, and no alternate cell-level crossing around it. This concerns removal of the crossing edges, not necessarily removal of one point.
